## v1.0

### Added
- **Password must have at least 10 characters**: The system now requires passwords to be at least 10 characters long for better security.

### Changed
- **Email must have @ and .**: The email validation now ensures that an email address contains both an "@" symbol and a "." symbol, making it more accurate.

- **Count uppercase vowels too**: The vowel counting function now includes both lowercase and uppercase vowels in its count.
